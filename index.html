Three Bedroom Double-Fronted End-of-Terrace Residence with Extensive Gardens, Double Garage & Outbuildings

An excellent opportunity to acquire a particularly appealing three-bedroom, double-fronted end-of-terrace residence occupying a generous site on Swords Road in the heart of Santry.

Dating from approximately 1930, this characterful single-storey home is set well back from the road behind an impressive mature front garden and enjoys an unusually broad frontage. Its end-of-terrace position provides valuable pedestrian access along the side of the house to the extensive rear garden.

Internally, the accommodation extends to approximately 95 sq. m. and offers a particularly versatile layout. The original accommodation is complemented by extensions to the rear, providing two separate reception rooms together with a generously proportioned kitchen/breakfast room.

The accommodation comprises an entrance hall, attractive front living room with bay window and fireplace, three bedrooms, lobby, bathroom/wet room, spacious rear lounge/family room and extended kitchen/breakfast room. The property benefits from oil-fired central heating.

Outside is where this property really distinguishes itself. In addition to the substantial gardens, there are two separate sheds/outbuildings together with an impressive double garage measuring approximately 5.7m x 5.5m. The garage is approached independently via a rear laneway providing direct vehicular access.

The combination of a generous site, accommodation arranged on one level, two reception rooms and extensive garage and storage facilities makes this a distinctive property with considerable appeal.
